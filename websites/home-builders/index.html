FAQ

Home builder website design, answered

What is a cinematic website for a home builder?
A cinematic website is a scroll-driven site where motion, video, and typography work together like a short film. For a home builder, each scroll reveals a home the way a walkthrough would — so the site feels directed rather than laid out as a static photo gallery.
How much does home builder website design cost?
A flat $700 one-time build fee plus a $99/month care plan that covers hosting, edits, and upkeep. No retainers, no hidden fees.
Do I have to pay before I see the website?
No. We build a live demo of your site first, in your real brand colours. You only pay if you want to launch it.
Can you match our existing brand and photography?
Yes. We build in your brand's real colours and use your project photography, so the site looks like an extension of your company rather than a template.
Will the website work on mobile?
Every site we build is mobile-responsive and tested on phones. The cinematic scroll experience adapts to smaller screens without losing performance.
What does the $99/month care plan include?
Hosting, domain handling, content edits, small updates, and ongoing monitoring and fixes. You can add new projects as you complete them, and cancel anytime.
Can I see a live example of your work?
Yes — our Hunter Express build is a live, engineering-grade cinematic site. We can share more relevant home-builder examples on request.
Where is Thrive Labs based?
Thrive Labs is based in Vancouver, BC, Canada, and works with home builders both locally and beyond.

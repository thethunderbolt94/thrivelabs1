FAQ

Vancouver home builder websites, answered

Do you design home builder websites in Vancouver?
Yes. Thrive Labs is a Vancouver, BC studio and works with luxury and custom home builders across the city, West Vancouver, the North Shore, and Whistler.
How much does home builder website design cost in Vancouver?
A flat $700 one-time build fee plus a $99/month care plan covering hosting, edits, and upkeep. No retainers or hidden fees.
Do I have to pay before I see the website?
No. We build a live demo of your site first, in your real brand colours. You only pay if you want to launch it.
Can you feature West Vancouver and Whistler projects properly?
Yes. Cinematic scroll pacing is built to show scale and setting — ideal for waterfront West Van builds, North Shore homes, and Whistler chalets where the location is part of the sell.
Will the website work on mobile?
Every site we build is mobile-responsive and tested on phones, with the cinematic scroll experience adapting to smaller screens without losing performance.
Do we need to meet in person?
No. We work with Vancouver builders remotely and can meet locally when it helps. Everything from brief to launch can be handled online.

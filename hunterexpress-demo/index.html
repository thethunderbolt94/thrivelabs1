Story · 2005 to today

Founded in 2005. Here is how we got here.

Scroll →

  1. 2005

    Founded in Brampton

    Hunter Express starts in Brampton, Ontario, in partnership with a 60-truck fleet. US authority follows in 2006.

  2. 2008

    Certified for the border

    C-TPAT and HAZMAT in 2008, FAST and PIP in 2010, ISO 9001 in 2011.

  3. 2014

    Windsor and Michigan terminals

    A terminal in Belle River, Ontario in 2014, with a full sales and safety department, then a fully staffed Michigan office in 2016 for cross-border and US runs.

  4. 2017

    Laredo terminal

    Five paved, fenced acres with a 40,000 sq ft bonded warehouse and an onsite US–Mexico customs broker. FCA’s “Extra Mile” award follows in 2019.

  5. 2021

    Into Mexico

    Terminals and offices open in Saltillo, Monterrey and Nuevo Laredo.

  6. 2025

    Supplier of the Year

    Named a Stellantis Global Supplier of the Year, Logistics, at the ceremony in Paris.

Temple of Speak

The room I built.

Temple of Speak is the 10-week, in-person program I founded in 2025. We meet three hours a week, about ten of us, and every session opens with a guided meditation.

The first seven weeks give emotions a voice, with voice control and body language. The last three are your story and a vision talk.

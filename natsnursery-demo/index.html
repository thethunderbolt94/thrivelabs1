    Custom growing & seed collection

    The plants you need, when you need them, from the seed source you specify.

    Reclamation and revegetation succeed or fail on provenance. NATS collects seed in your project region, holds it in our seed bank with records, and grows it out on your schedule. We have grown from Inuvik to the California coast.

    How a custom grow works

    From seed to site in five steps

    1 · BriefSpecies list, counts, sizes, planting date, provenance zone
    2 · SeedFrom your collection, our seed bank, or a NATS collection trip
    3 · ContractGrow plan and deposit. 1 to 2 seasons depending on species
    4 · GrowStratified, sown, inoculated, hardened off
    5 · ShipPalletised and delivered, with records

    About NATS

    We grow the lungs of the world.

    Founded by Rod Nataros in 1988 as North American Tree Specialists, NATS moved to a purpose-built 30-acre nursery in Langley in 2002. Today a team of growers, propagators and salespeople produce over 300 native species for restoration, reclamation, reforestation and green roofs.

    The NATS story

    1988Founded on Fraser Highway in Surrey as North American Tree Specialists.
    2002Moved to 30 acres in Langley. New propagation facility, greenhouses, shipping yard.
    2006Awarded the Vancouver Convention Centre growing contract: 350,000 plants.
    2010sBecame BC's licensed LiveRoof grower and Permaloc distributor. Began Christmas tree seedling program.
    2018Celebrated 30 years. AgSafe COR certification followed in 2024.
    2025Launched Gather and Grow seed collection training.

    Indigenous relations

    We conduct our business on the traditional lands of the Katzie, Kwantlen, Matsqui and Semiahmoo First Nations. We believe reclamation should return land to functioning habitat for wildlife and people, and that this requires the knowledge of the original inhabitants. NATS is proud to work with First Nations on land reclamation across BC, the North and Alberta.

    Shipping

    Regular NATS truck routes cover the Lower Mainland, Fraser Valley, Sea to Sky and Vancouver Island. Pick-up at the nursery welcome with 24 hours notice.

    • Minimum order applies to truck delivery
    • LiveRoof delivered on returnable racks
    • Plugs ship in trays; containers on pallets

    Common carrier or dedicated truck to Alberta, the Yukon, NWT and beyond. Dormant stock ships best in spring and fall.

    • Provincial plant health requirements handled by NATS
    • Custom grows scheduled around your planting window

Our story · The human behind the café

I’m Amrin.I’m Amrin.

You N Me’ Café was started by one person, for a fairly specific reason.

0102030405 / 05

01 / 05

I arrived with big dreams and a heavy suitcase.

I came to Canada from India in 2011 as an international student. I worked until my bones ached.

02 / 05

There’s a particular kind of alone that nobody talks about.

It’s sitting in a café on a Saturday, scrolling your phone so it looks like you’re waiting for someone.

03 / 05

The same date, twice.

Jul178201289012345

On July 17, 2018, the day before my birthday, I was asked to leave Canada. On July 18, 2025, my birthday, I signed the papers for a café at 921 12th Street in New Westminster.

04 / 05

So I built the place I’d wanted to walk into.

Not a support group. Not a service. But a café where coming alone is normal. Where somebody remembers you.

05 / 05

This isn’t just a business. It’s my heart, open to yours.

So this is that place. A sanctuary for your sadness and a stage for your happiness.

Welcome home.

— AmrinAmrin

Good to know

  • Parking

    Street parking along 12th Street, usually something within a block. Check the posted signs for time limits.

  • Getting here by transit

    New Westminster SkyTrain station is a short bus ride or a walk away, with several bus routes running along and near 12th Street.

  • Accessibility

    Ring 672-335-6858 or email us and I will tell you exactly what the entrance and the room are like before you come.

    Accessible washroom

  • Free Wi-Fi

    Bring your laptop, a book, a journal or the latest idea. Board games and books are on the shelf and free to use. Wi-Fi is free. Nobody will rush you.
